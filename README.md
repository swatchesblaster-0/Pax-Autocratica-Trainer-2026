## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Pax Autocratica**. This trainer enhances the colony-to-combat gameplay loop by providing optional control over player survivability, ammunition, resources, citizen management, and roguelite progression. It is designed for experimentation, relaxed play, and testing different regimes, weapons, squads, and colony strategies.

**Target Audience:** Suitable for players who want to experiment with colony layouts, citizen policies, production chains, weapon builds, squad tactics, and chaotic first-person battles without normal resource or survival restrictions.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Infinite Health** — Prevents the player from losing health during first-person expeditions.
- **Unlimited Resources** — Prevents selected colony resources and supplies from decreasing during construction, production, research, and preparation.
- **Instant Construction** — Completes supported colony construction tasks immediately.
- **Instant Production** — Finishes supported manufacturing tasks without waiting for their normal production time.
- **Citizen Stability** — Helps maintain citizen loyalty and reduces the impact of fear, unrest, and rebellion-related pressures.
- **Unlimited Roguelite Cores** — Keeps collected cores available for experimenting with weapon, combination, and troop-enhancing builds.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Unlimited Ammunition** — Provides unlimited ammunition for equipped weapons during battlefield encounters.
- **No Reload** — Removes reload delays while using firearms and other reload-based weapons.
- **Unlimited Squad Survivability** — Protects allied soldiers from ordinary battlefield damage while enabled.
- **Weak Enemies** — Reduces enemy durability to make infantry, vehicles, mechs, bosses, and other threats easier to defeat.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Launch the game normally, load a single-player colony, and activate movement-related or survivability options only after gaining control of the player character. Use the trainer to explore hostile territories, gather resources, recruit or capture potential citizens, and return safely to the colony. Disable temporary options before transitioning between colony management and expeditions if unexpected behavior occurs.

### Tools & Inventory

Enter the colony or an expedition before enabling resource, ammunition, or core options. Unlimited ammunition supports the game's weapon-focused first-person combat, while resource protection assists with construction, research, manufacturing, food, armor, and supplies. Roguelite core options are intended for testing weapon handling changes, combination synergies, and troop upgrades acquired during expeditions.

### World & Environment

Pax Autocratica connects colony management with expeditions across the fractured territories of the Tyris System. Citizens can mine, gather, haul, research, and manufacture materials while the player expands the settlement. Use the trainer to test different work assignments, construction plans, production priorities, policies, and approaches to recruiting or converting captured enemies.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat takes place in first-person roguelite battlefields featuring infantry, vehicles, tanks, mechs, drones, artillery, lasers, missiles, bosses, and Sector Overlords. Activate health, ammunition, squad, or enemy options after entering a battle. Experiment with direct weapon use, troop orders, expendable shields, captured enemies, and different core combinations. Turn off combat options before the end of a run to reduce the chance of progression or reward issues.
